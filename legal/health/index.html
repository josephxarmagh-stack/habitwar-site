Consumer Health Data

Last updated October 17, 2026

This is our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, for Washington, Nevada and other US states with health data laws. It is part of our Privacy Policy and is also published at gethabitwar.com/legal/health. What we collect • Apple Health daily totals, only if you turn on Save my daily totals. • Oura readings, only if you connect your Oura ring. • Check-ins of habits that track your health, and habit names or notes that say something about your health (for example a habit to take medicine). Where it comes from You, Apple Health on your iPhone, and Oura. Why we use it Only to check off and show your habits and your own history. Who gets it • Supabase, the database provider that stores it for us. • People who can see a habit (your choice in Who can see my habits, and the members of a war it is in): they see its name, its check-ins and any note or proof photo you add, never your Health or Oura numbers. • Apple and Google, which deliver notifications: a notification can name a habit and a streak, never your Health or Oura numbers. We never sell health data and never use it for ads or marketing. Your consent • Apple Health totals and Oura readings: only after you say yes — on Apple's Health permission screen, to the Save my daily totals question, and with Sign in with Oura. • Habit names, notes and check-ins: when you add them, to run the app you asked for. Your rights You can see your data, delete it, or take back your consent at any time: turn off Save my daily totals or disconnect Oura in Settings → Connections, delete a habit or your account, or email us at support@gethabitwar.com. We answer within 30 days.
