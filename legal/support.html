Support

Last updated October 17, 2026

Habit War is made by one independent developer, Nicholas Piva. He reads every message. Email: support@gethabitwar.com Report a person or content In the app: hold a message and tap Report, or use Report on a person, post, comment, photo or war. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks our Terms is removed, and so is the person who posted it. In danger? Call your local emergency number first. Everything else Bugs, login problems, questions about your data, ideas: email us. We aim to reply within 2 business days. Include your username or account email, your iPhone model and iOS version, the app version (bottom of Settings), what happened, and a screenshot if you can. Your data • A copy: Settings → Export & backup. For anything it doesn't include, email us. • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account. It removes your profile, habits, check-ins, photos, friends, war memberships, the messages you sent, your conversations with friends, reactions and device tokens. Reports, automatic war lines and backups follow our Privacy Policy (section 8).
